Journal of Readable Systems • Vol. VII

On White Space as Active Rhythm

Opening Argument — Readers perceive margins before words.[1] When line lengths exceed seventy-five characters, the eye repeatedly loses its anchor during the return sweep between lines.

Generous vertical spacing, coupled with subtle typographic markers and restrained pull citations,[2] sustains comprehension across dense long-form narratives.

[1] Bringhurst, Robert. The Elements of Typographic Style (4th ed.), Chapter 2: Rhythm & Proportion, p. 26.

[2] Optimal digital reading measure sits between 58ch and 68ch at 16–18px.
